Welcome to KCC

A Legacy of Faith & Education

Founded in 1927, KCC has served as a home of Catholic education in Kabankalan City. The College forms learners who are grounded in faith, strong in academics, and ready to serve their communities.

Our guiding motto: “Teach Ye All Nations” — Matthew 28:19.
